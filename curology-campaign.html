Influencer Strategy

Not skincare influencers.
Lifestyle influencers who happen to have skin.

🏃‍♀️

Fitness & Run Club Creators

Run clubs are everywhere right now. Pilates. The gym. Everyone is working on their body — and wants their face to match. Sweating daily creates real skin concerns: breakouts, irritation, disrupted barrier. This makes the product feel actively necessary, not aspirational.

🌿

Wellness "It Girl" Creators

The girl who talks about gut health, her minerals, her matcha — she's the same girl who is intentional about her skincare. Curology's personalized model fits her identity perfectly. She's not buying random products off a shelf. She researches. She optimizes. Curology is her brand.

🔬

Dermatologist Creators

Dermatologist TikTok is a massive, trusted category. These creators already have the audience Curology needs — people who are curious about ingredients, want clinical truth, and will actually try what a derm recommends. This is credibility that can't be faked or purchased.

📵

Why Not Traditional Skincare Influencers

Skincare influencers are oversaturated with brand deals, and their audiences have learned to tune out product placements. Reaching Curology's target through fitness and wellness creators means the product discovery feels organic — not like an ad they've already seen 40 times.
